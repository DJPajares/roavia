# Travel-data provider and licensing matrix

- Linear issue: [WDL-28](https://linear.app/wwonderland/issue/WDL-28/research-travel-data-providers-and-licensing)
- Researched: 2026-07-28
- Status: Candidate shortlist; not a provider, budget, contract, or launch-catalog approval
- Decision owner: Darwin Jason Pajares

## Purpose

This record evaluates candidate sources for Roavia's destination, place, map,
route, weather, climate, holiday, event, advisory, visa, emergency, currency,
and media data. It applies the provider boundaries in
[ADR 0003](../architecture/decisions/0003-provider-integration-boundaries.md)
and the privacy lifecycle in
[ADR 0005](../architecture/decisions/0005-sensitive-data-lifecycle.md).

The shortlist is intentionally composable. No candidate is assumed to cover
every category, and no fallback is interchangeable unless its normalized
fields, coverage, freshness, license, privacy, and offline rights match the
primary source. Prices and service terms are observations as of the research
date and must be checked again before procurement.

## Launch coverage gates

The concrete cities, countries, and languages belong to WDL-57. Until that
decision exists, each category must meet these provisional coverage gates for
every selected launch destination:

| Category                | Required launch coverage                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Places and hierarchy    | Every launch place resolves to stable country, region, city, and relevant district/POI identifiers, localized names, coordinates, time zone, category, operating state, and provenance.                                                  |
| Destination editorial   | Every launch destination has a reviewed source set for overview, practical information, customs, language, money, transport, accessibility, safety, visa links, and emergency contacts. Missing optional prose is explicit.              |
| Maps and routes         | Every launch city supports map display, forward/reverse geocoding, and walking and driving estimates. Transit is optional until a separately licensed feed is approved.                                                                  |
| Weather and climate     | Every launch destination supports current conditions, forecast, precipitation, wind, severe-weather source links, and historical or climatological seasonality.                                                                          |
| Holidays and events     | Every launch country has national holiday coverage; subdivisions are required where local holidays affect a selected destination. Every destination has at least one official event/festival source even when no structured feed exists. |
| Safety, visa, emergency | Every supported traveler nationality/residency and destination pair has an official visa/entry path. Every destination has official advisory, police, fire, ambulance, and consular links or is not launch-ready.                        |
| Currency                | Every launch currency has a dated indicative rate against the user's preferred currency, or the product clearly marks conversion unavailable.                                                                                            |
| Media                   | Every launch destination has enough reviewed assets to satisfy WDL-57, with creator, source page, license, attribution, modification, and offline-use metadata recorded per file.                                                        |

An evaluation suite should cover, without pre-approving these as launch
destinations, at least these scenarios:

- Singapore: tropical weather, city-state hierarchy, regional holidays, and
  Singapore-authority advisory paths.
- Tokyo: non-Latin localization, dense rail context, districts, and high POI
  density.
- Paris: historic-city pedestrian routes, multilingual names, and
  share-alike/open media.
- New York City: large North American hierarchy, driving/transit distinction,
  and high request volume.
- Sydney: Southern Hemisphere seasonality, coastal weather, and long-haul
  route context.
- Reykjavik plus a secondary Southeast Asian city: sparse/seasonal routes,
  accessibility, budget variation, and uneven commercial-provider coverage.

## Normalized data requirements

Every adapter must emit the common provenance fields required by ADR 0003:
provider and source URL, provider record ID, retrieved/published/valid/expiry
timestamps, license and attribution, offline permission, locale and region
coverage, quality/confidence, and cost units.

Category-specific minimums are:

| Category                | Required normalized fields                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Place                   | Stable ID; place type; localized names; parent hierarchy; coordinates; time zone; country code; address; categories; operating status; official website; phone/hours/accessibility when supplied; match confidence. |
| Editorial               | Destination and section; locale; source segments; author/authority; review state; factual validity; change history; citations.                                                                                      |
| Map/geocode/route       | Query and matched precision; normalized address; coordinates; route mode; waypoints; geometry or approved representation; distance; duration; restrictions; traffic basis; map attribution.                         |
| Weather/climate         | Coordinates and time zone; model/source; model run and valid time; temperature; precipitation; humidity; wind; UV; alert authority; forecast horizon; historical/climate period.                                    |
| Holiday/event           | Name; type; jurisdiction; local start/end and time zone; venue/location; organizer; official URL; recurrence; status/cancellation; ticket URL where applicable.                                                     |
| Advisory/visa/emergency | Destination; traveler nationality/residency inputs; issuing authority; advisory level/scope; issued/updated times; official summary/link; entry-document rule and validity; official emergency/consular contacts.   |
| Currency                | Base and quote currency; rate; as-of time; reference/market basis; provider; informational-use warning.                                                                                                             |
| Media                   | Asset and creator IDs; source page and delivery URL; dimensions; license name/version/URL; attribution text; modification flag; model/personality/trademark caveats; offline permission; takedown state.            |

Fields unsupported by a provider remain `unknown`; adapters must not infer
them. Provider-specific richness may be retained in namespaced metadata but
cannot silently change product behavior.

Official-source behavior is non-negotiable:

1. Resolve visa/entry rules from the traveler's supported nationality and
   residency plus destination/transit context; do not generalize one
   nationality's rule to another.
2. Preserve authority, URL, published/updated/retrieved timestamps, affected
   region, and exact validity period.
3. Poll machine-readable sources no less frequently than the freshness policy
   below permits, but obey source throttling and robots rules.
4. When an official source is unavailable, conflicting, or stale, show
   `unavailable`, `conflicting`, or `stale`, direct the traveler to the
   authority, and require manual review. Do not lower severity or merge
   advisory levels automatically.
5. Treat emergency numbers and consular contacts as curated records reviewed
   against the destination authority before publication and each offline
   refresh.
6. AI may summarize a captured official record with a citation and uncertainty
   label; it may not invent, complete, or overrule a rule.

## Freshness and cache policy

These are Roavia's maximum defaults; a provider's stricter contract or HTTP
cache directive always wins.

| Data                      | Refresh target                                                                                                                                      | Stale behavior                                                                                                                                                 | Offline rule                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open place catalog        | Ingest each monthly release/delta; high-value launch places receive at least quarterly editorial review.                                            | Show last verified time; suppress closed/merged records after the next successful delta.                                                                       | Include only fields whose source chain permits redistribution, with an attribution manifest.                          |
| Live place details/hours  | Cache no longer than the provider allows; target 24 hours for ordinary details and shorter around a planned visit.                                  | Mark hours/closure unknown rather than carrying an expired assertion.                                                                                          | Exclude restricted enrichments unless the contract explicitly grants offline display.                                 |
| Geocodes                  | Session-only for temporary results; indefinite only for paid permanent results or approved open data.                                               | Re-resolve only through the same rights class; never convert a temporary result into stored data.                                                              | Include coordinates/address only when storage and end-user delivery are permitted.                                    |
| Routes/maps               | Cache according to service headers/contract. Traffic-aware duration expires after 15 minutes; static route estimates after 24 hours.                | Keep itinerary waypoints but label route duration unavailable or stale.                                                                                        | No Mapbox web tiles by default. Open tiles/routes need ODbL attribution and approved derived-database design.         |
| Forecast/weather alerts   | Destination forecast cache 30 minutes; official severe alerts 15 minutes or provider feed cadence.                                                  | Forecast stale after two hours; alert feed stale after one hour. Show source and last update, never silently reuse expired live data.                          | A downloaded forecast expires; climatology can remain with period/source. CC BY attribution travels with the package. |
| Climate normals           | Refresh monthly or when the upstream dataset/model version changes.                                                                                 | Keep period/model visible; do not present climatology as a forecast.                                                                                           | Permitted only under source license.                                                                                  |
| Holidays                  | Refresh yearly data monthly, daily during the 30 days before travel, and immediately on official change notice.                                     | Mark provisional/changed dates and link the authority.                                                                                                         | Include only with confirmed redistribution rights and an as-of date.                                                  |
| Events                    | Official feed cadence or six hours for live status; manual calendars reviewed weekly and again 72 hours before a saved event.                       | Expired or canceled events are suppressed; uncertain status is explicit.                                                                                       | Include event facts only when source terms allow it; live inventory/prices stay online-only.                          |
| Advisories/visa/emergency | Check official feeds at least hourly, faster when an authority offers push/change feeds; manually re-verify before publication and offline refresh. | High-stakes summary becomes stale after six hours or at its source validity boundary. Preserve official links and show unavailable rather than fallback prose. | Snapshot only when reuse is lawful; always store retrieval/expiry and warn users to recheck online.                   |
| Currency                  | Poll after the provider's publication time; daily is sufficient for MVP.                                                                            | Stale after two business days; conversion becomes unavailable rather than pretending to be current.                                                            | Store dated reference rates with attribution and informational warning.                                               |
| Media rights              | Validate at ingestion, before publication, and on takedown/change signal.                                                                           | Unpublish on license uncertainty or takedown while retaining the audit record.                                                                                 | Package only assets with explicit offline rights and frozen attribution/license metadata.                             |

Provider calls are server-side and centrally cached by destination/time bucket.
They must not include user IDs, free-text itinerary notes, companion details, or
exact user location. A request may use the minimum destination coordinate and
time needed for the result.

## Cost comparison

The following workload is only a comparison unit, not a forecast or budget:
10,000 monthly active users, 100,000 web map loads, 200,000 directions calls,
10,000 stored geocodes, 30 launch weather points refreshed every 30 minutes,
and one holiday/currency refresh per source period.

| Category             | Indicative monthly cost at the comparison unit                                                                                                                                         | Important exclusions                                                                                                  |
